## Notes

- The original software category is too broad; operating-system, application, update, driver, activation, and setup issues should be analyzed separately at drill-down level.
- Screen and display were merged, while external-monitor and dock issues were placed with external equipment so built-in display defects remain measurable.
- Power-on, charging, battery, power cord, shutdown, and power-management labels were consolidated under Power & Charging because the sample does not require separate top-level reporting for each.
- Repair case, support case, support line, support transfer, and technical-support labels describe service operations rather than a product component and belong in Repair & Service Case.
- The sample contains several unsupported or misrouted requests, including human resources and some tablet or server requests; these are retained in Other / Unclear when no Lenovo PC service area is defensible.
